Who is your worst enemy?

It seems like a simple question. Who is someone that is out to get you, clawing and fighting for your downfall. In our modern era, I don't think mortal enemies exist anymore. Even then, they seem to be more a literary device than anything. I know for sure that I don't have one.

Or do I?

I have a saying that I like. Its simple and to the point. a good quote too. If your interested lol. Its something saved for when friends are having a tough time, Or when someone is just in a slump.

You, are your own worst enemy. The only person who is stopping you from chasing your dreams and fighting for your future is yourself. No one else makes you doomscroll for hours, and no one else makes you procrastinate. And It seems so simple and obvious. But fighting yourself Is no simple task.

For the most part, I write these more personal articles as something that I'd have liked to read as my younger self. To be honest, Its alright to be in your own way. Fighting yourself is hard. No one can wake up one day and just decide to be better. change is a slow, and very stoppable force.

Self-sabotage is often subconscious, and when you are deep in your routine It can be hard to sit up, look around and start naming things to change.

Contentedness breeds complacency.

Never stop moving, pushing, striding towards a perfect life for yourself. If you take that moment, to look around and just say "I am my own worst enemy" things will start to change. A problem can't be solved if you don't know what it is. Be happy, but never be complacent.

For me, My largest point of change is change itself. Momentum is key, and once you get going, coasting takes no mental effort. One big thing is how you treat your monkey brain. I like to think of this as a sort of two layer "piloting" system, where your conscience tells your monkey brain what to do, which in turn tells your body what to do. and every little thing that happens in a day is a vote of sorts for how things are going to go. each vote for discipline makes hard decisions easier. by hard i do mean not going on Instagram, eating junk or other things.

the cold shower does work. not fun, but works.

It is ok to mess up. don't keep streaks, don't quantify failure or success. you are doing great, as long as you try. every attempt is a win.
