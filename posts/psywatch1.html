July 27th 2026

Alright, a lot to go over in not that much space. If you haven't watched the first part youtube video I made, that's a good spot to start from.

A little refresher:

In high school, i made a "smart" watch that really never worked. the actual watch part was just a watch i had already, and it ended up just being a screen that told temperature and turned a flashlight on and off. not that exciting. I do still see this project as a success because its what got me into making things like this. The first version (i call it MK one (MK1)) used the raspberry pi foundations RP2040 chip on a Pico, powered from a 9v battery and with one of those 16x2 character LCD panels that come in every starter kit ever. the one thing i did succeed in was price, adding things up, the total was less than 30$ if i bought a RTC. last issues is style, It looks like an explosive. ever see the bomb from CS2? it has the same screen. all i was missing was the curly wires.

Design doc:

For the Psywatch MK2, I want a few things:

  • Gotta be cheap, less than $50.
  • wide range of connectivity (wifi, bluetooth, RF)
  • Practical / easy to use
  • needs some useful features (flashlight, IR LED / detector)
  • Visually interesting but also out of the way. (wear in public)
  • lastly, it needs to be a good platform to build off of.

my goal is to have enough hardware features that I can expand functionality with a few more lines of code.

more specific requirements:

  • last a full two days on one charge
  • some sort of automatic and manual screen off feature (gesture control)
